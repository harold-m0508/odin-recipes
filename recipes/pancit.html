Pancit Bihon

Description

Pancit bihon is a classic Filipino noodle dish that comes together with minimal effort. Tender rice noodles are tossed with a colorful mix of vegetables and chicken, all coated in a perfectly balanced sweet and savory sauce. Best of all, this crowd-pleasing recipe is ready in just 25 minutes from start to finish.

Engredients

  • Peanut Oil: A staple in many Asian dishes, peanut oil lends a subtle nuttiness to the recipe. That said, any neutral oil such as vegetable or grapeseed oil works just as well if that's what you have on hand.
  • Chicken Breast: Boneless, skinless chicken breast is ideal here since it needs to be cut into bite-sized pieces. If you prefer bone-in, skin-on chicken, feel free to use it — simply cook it separately in a pan until fully done, then follow the same steps in the recipe.
  • Garlic: Two minced cloves add a gentle kick of pungency to the dish. If you're a garlic enthusiast, go ahead and add more to your liking.
  • Onion: A small yellow onion, finely chopped, works perfectly here. No yellow onion? White, red, or even shallots are all great stand-ins.
  • Bihon Noodles: These thin noodles are made from a blend of cornstarch and rice flour, giving them a light, delicate texture. Thin rice noodles or vermicelli are the closest substitutes if bihon isn't available.
  • Mixed Vegetables: Green beans, carrots, and cabbage make up the vegetable base here, but feel free to mix and match with whatever you have or love.
  • Pancit Sauce: A flavorful blend of low-sodium chicken stock, dark soy sauce, regular soy sauce, oyster sauce, and a touch of sugar for balance.
  • Salt and Pepper: Taste as you go and only add if the dish needs a little extra seasoning.

Steps

  1. Sauce. Whisk all the sauce ingredients together in a bowl until well combined and set aside.
  2. Chicken. Heat a drizzle of oil in a deep skillet over medium heat and cook the chicken until done. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
  3. Garlic and onions. In the same skillet, sauté the garlic and onion until softened and aromatic.
  4. Cooked chicken and veggies. Add the chicken back into the skillet along with the vegetables. Stir-fry until the veggies are tender but still have a satisfying bite.
  5. Add the sauce. Give the sauce a quick whisk, pour it into the skillet, and bring everything to a boil.
  6. Cook the noodles. Add the rice noodles and gently press them down into the liquid so they absorb it evenly. Cook until the noodles are soft and fully tender.
  7. Season and serve. Remove from heat and transfer the pancit to a serving bowl or plate. Serve alongside soy sauce and fresh lemon wedges.
